Drop off your e-waste, get paid instantly, and know it ends up recycled responsibly. Simple as that.

// Our Commitment

Circular by design.
Zero e-waste. Always.

We believe a component's life shouldn't end in a hostel drawer or a bin. Magnemite is built on one promise: nothing we touch ends up in a landfill.

♻️ The Zero E-Waste Guarantee

Components that fail our QA tests are not discarded — they're aggregated by material type and routed to authorised B2B recyclers and certified smelters who recover copper, tin, gold, and rare-earth metals from PCBs. Every batch comes with a recycling manifest so you know exactly where your old hardware ended up.

The Business Case

Why Institutional Feedstock Wins

Household e-waste collection is expensive, contaminated, and unpredictable. Campus aggregation is the opposite.

The Old Model
Scattered Household Pickup
  • ✗Dozens of micro-pickups per tonne — last-mile logistics eat 30–40% of margin
  • ✗Contaminated streams mixed with household plastics and food residue
  • ✗Unpredictable volume — can't forecast feedstock for furnace scheduling
  • ✗High manual sorting cost before material hits your processing line
The Magnemite Model
Campus Aggregation Node
  • ✓One truck pickup per batch — full bulk consignment from a single centralized node
  • ✓Pre-segregated, uncontaminated streams — STEM hardware only, zero domestic waste
  • ✓Scheduled dispatch windows — predictable feedstock for your furnace planning
  • ✓Zero sorting overhead — arrives weighed, labelled, and categorized by type
